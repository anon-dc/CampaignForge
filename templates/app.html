Authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)

These DNS records prove your mail is legitimate:

SPF
v=spf1 include:_spf.yourprovider.com ~all
DKIM
selector._domainkey TXT "v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=..."
DMARC
_dmarc TXT "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:you@domain.com"
